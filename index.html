Opening it the first time

  1. Unzip Panda.zip and drag Panda into your Applications folder.
  2. macOS blocks apps that aren't from the App Store. Open the Terminal app (Cmd+Space, type Terminal), paste this, and press Enter:
    xattr -cr /Applications/Panda.app
  3. Open Panda from Applications. If it's still blocked, go to System Settings → Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click Open Anyway.
  4. Click the panda to add your tasks. To quit, click ⚙ then Quit Panda.
